Who We Are

Nexus Acquisition ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Nexus Outbound Engine, a done-for-you cold outbound service. Our website is nexusacquisitionai.com. This policy explains how we handle information collected through this site and our service engagement process.

Information We Collect

When you submit an application through our strategy call form, we collect the information you provide, which may include:

  • —Your name and business email address
  • —Your company name, website, and industry

  • —Information about your current sales process and pipeline goals

We do not collect payment information through this site. Billing is handled through secure third-party processors.

How We Use Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

Data Retention

We retain your application information for as long as necessary to evaluate your inquiry and, if applicable, manage our client relationship. If you do not become a client, your information is retained for no longer than 12 months from the date of submission, after which it is deleted.

Your Rights
